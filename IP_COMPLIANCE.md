# Icon Design & IP Compliance Audit

This document records the provenance and intellectual property (IP) clearance process for the "Fluffy" application icon.

## 1. Overview & Provenance
- **Asset:** Application Icon and Assets for "Fluffy" (Video Wallpaper Manager for COSMIC DE).
- **Generation Method:** AI-assisted synthesis (via Google Gemini) iteratively refined to prevent collusion and ensure architectural originality.
- **Style:** Non-glossy, matte finish compliant with modern desktop environment (DE) design languages.

## 2. Competitive Visual Search & IP Clearance
An image-based visual search and trademark schema check were performed to eliminate accidental infringement risks.

| Target / Brand | Design Comparison | Risk Assessment & Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adobe Creative Cloud** | Flowing lines forming a loop. | **Low**: The "Fluffy" icon uniquely synthesizes the literal character "F" intertwined with the official COSMIC DE logo. Distinguishable by its flat matte style and strict typographic purpose. No likelihood of consumer confusion. |
| **Papyrus** | App category competitor (COSMIC video wallpaper manager). | **Clear Distinction**: "Papyrus" uses a scroll/paper motif. "Fluffy" incorporates the official COSMIC DE branding into its core glyph, providing clear visual differentiation within the COSMIC Store ecosystem. |

## 3. Final Compliance Assessment
- **Originality:** The icon successfully integrates three non-generic elements: the unique "F" glyph frame, the functional display/playback glyph, and the integrated COSMIC DE branding.
- **Copyright Status:** No copyrighted or trademarked assets were directly copied. The asset is approved for commercial/open-source usage within the Fluffy project.
